About TMH

TMH started in 2020 as a Discord server for current, future, and former transgender US service members. Since then it has expanded to multiple platforms and is a cornerstone for the community. We provide help and resources along with a place to hang out with friends virtually. In the constantly changing landscape of transgender rights, and protections, TMH is there for the community.

What You'll Find Inside

Community

A place to spend time and chat with other transgender service members. Play video games together, share music recommendations, and share the funniest meme of the day.

Mutual Aid

When a participant in one of our programs is in crisis, we work together to help them the best we can. We try to arm everyone with the resources they need or point them to the place to get the resources we don't have.

Advocacy

We use the resources out there to help track state and local level legislation impacting the transgender military community; both positively and negatively. We encourage everyone to participate in advocacy efforts that will help protect all transgender individuals.

Joining the Community

We verify new members to keep bad actors out. If you are interested in joining, we promise that your safety, security and privacy matters and will be protected.
